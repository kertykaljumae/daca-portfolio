# Week 6: Data Visualization – Power BI (E-commerce Dashboard)

## What I Did

- Created an interactive Power BI dashboard focusing on e-commerce sales performance and growth opportunities.
- Analyzed online revenue, order volume, average order value, and year-over-year growth.
- Visualized monthly sales trends, e-commerce revenue share, website traffic sources, and TOP 5 products.
- Added a year slicer, KPI cards, and annotations to highlight key insights.
- Prepared a data narrative and executive summary with business recommendations.

## Key Findings

- **Revenue:** €536.2K in 2024, increasing by 37.9% compared to 2023.
- **Orders:** Increased by 39.7%, while average order value decreased by 1.4% to €288.
- **E-commerce share:** Grew from 31.5% in 2023 to 36.5% in 2024.
- **Sales trends:** December had the highest monthly revenue (€63.2K), while June showed approximately 50% growth compared to May.
- **Website traffic:** Google Organic was the leading traffic source, followed by Direct and Facebook Ads.

## Power BI Design Decisions

- **KPI cards:** Used to highlight revenue, order volume, average order value, and year-over-year changes.
- **Line chart:** Used to visualize monthly revenue trends and identify sales peaks.
- **Stacked bar chart:** Used to compare e-commerce revenue share between 2023 and 2024.
- **Horizontal bar charts:** Used to compare website traffic sources and TOP 5 products by revenue.
- **Year slicer:** Added to enable interactive filtering.
- **Annotations and reference line:** Used to emphasize significant growth periods and the 2025 e-commerce share target.
- **Layout and formatting:** Applied a clean design, consistent colors, and short explanatory notes to improve readability.

## Recommendations

- Increase average order value through cross-selling and product bundles.
- Strengthen organic search visibility through SEO optimization.
- Analyze seasonal sales peaks to improve future marketing campaigns.
- Focus on increasing the e-commerce share towards the 2025 target of 45%.

## Key Learnings

- Practiced building an interactive e-commerce dashboard in Power BI.
- Improved my skills in KPI visualization, year-over-year comparisons, and dashboard storytelling.
- Learned to highlight business growth opportunities through data narratives and visual annotations.
- Practiced translating analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.
