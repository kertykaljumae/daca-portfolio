# Week 6 – Data Narrative: Growing E-commerce, New Opportunities

## Objective
To analyze e-commerce sales performance, growth trends, and website traffic sources to identify opportunities for further growth.

## Key Findings

**1. Strong e-commerce growth**

In 2024, e-commerce revenue reached €536.2K, increasing by 37.9% compared to the previous year. The number of orders grew by 39.7%, while the average order value decreased by 1.4% to €288.

**2. Increasing importance of e-commerce**

The share of e-commerce in total company revenue increased from 31.5% in 2023 to 36.5% in 2024, highlighting its growing contribution to overall sales.

**3. Strong seasonal sales performance**

In June, revenue increased by approximately 50% compared to May. The highest monthly revenue was recorded in December, reaching €63.2K.

**4. Organic search drives website traffic**

Google Organic is the largest source of website traffic, followed by Direct and Facebook Ads. This highlights the importance of organic search visibility.

## Conclusions and Recommendations

E-commerce growth is primarily driven by an increasing number of orders rather than higher average order values.

To support further growth, the company should focus on increasing basket size through cross-selling and product bundles.

Additionally, maintaining strong organic search visibility and analyzing successful sales periods could help identify opportunities for future campaigns.
