# Week 6 – Executive Summary

## Growing E-commerce, New Opportunities

### 2024 Key Performance Indicators

- **Revenue:** €536.2K (+37.9% YoY)
- **Number of orders:** 1.9K (+39.7% YoY)
- **Average order value:** €288 (−1.4% YoY)
- **E-commerce share of total revenue:** 36.5% (+5 percentage points)

## Key Insights

E-commerce is becoming an increasingly important growth driver for the company. Revenue and order volume are growing strongly, but the slight decline in average order value indicates an opportunity to increase basket size.

Google Organic is the leading source of website traffic, while June and December show particularly strong sales performance.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Increase average order value** – Introduce cross-selling strategies and product bundles to encourage customers to purchase more items per order.
2. **Strengthen organic visibility** – Continue SEO optimization and analyze how website traffic converts into purchases.
3. **Optimize seasonal campaigns** – Use insights from June and December sales peaks to improve future marketing campaigns.
4. **Increase e-commerce contribution** – Work towards the 2025 target of a 45% e-commerce share of total revenue.

## Conclusion

E-commerce demonstrates strong growth and an increasing contribution to company revenue. The next opportunity lies in increasing average order value and improving the effectiveness of existing traffic sources.
